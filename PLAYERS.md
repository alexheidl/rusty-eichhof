THE LAST EICHHOF - a port of the 1993 freeware shoot-'em-up by ALPHA-HELIX

This download contains only the new program. It needs the data of the original game:

1. Find BEER.DAT in your copy of The Last Eichhof (the DOS game folder).
2. Copy BEER.DAT into the same folder as this program
   (web version: next to index.html, then open the folder through a web server).
3. Optional: also copy CONFIG.HIG there to keep your old high scores and keys.
4. Start the program.

Keys: arrows move, Space fires (hold), P pauses, Esc gives up.
Touch screens: drag on the right half to move, hold the left half to fire.
